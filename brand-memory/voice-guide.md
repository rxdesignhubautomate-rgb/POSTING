# Voice guide

- `rx-global`: the RX team speaking to pharma marketers. Practical, confident and specific. Use “we”.
- `va-lucknow`: a warm local shop owner. Natural Lucknow Hinglish, places and useful coordination detail. Use “hum”.
- `sk-personal`: Shubham in first person. Honest, slightly informal, including what he tried and what failed.

1. Start with a concrete situation, supplied number, mistake or a real MR question. Never start with “In today’s…”, “Are you looking for…” or a generic question.
2. Mix sentence lengths. Some lines can be four words; others can be longer.
3. Keep one idea per post. Two ideas should become two posts.
4. Use real pharma vocabulary where it fits: MR, ASM, detailing, division, PCD, launch, doctor call, chemist and stockist.
5. Hinglish should sound naturally typed in Lucknow, not formal or forced.
6. Use at most two emojis per post, never at the start of every line, and none in YouTube titles.
7. Prefer full stops and line breaks to em dashes.
8. Include one practitioner detail such as GSM choice, lamination, page sequencing, division colour logic or a proof round only when it is supported by the brief.
9. End with one CTA, not three.
10. Never claim awards, rankings, certifications or No.1 status.
